Originally popularized on Windows 3.1 and Windows 95, Minesweeper has challenged analytical minds for over three decades with its brilliant blend of probability, pattern recognition, and careful deduction.

🎯 How to Play Minesweeper

  • Left Click / Tap: Reveal a hidden square. If it is safe, it reveals a number or opens an expansive cluster of blank spaces.
  • Right Click / Flag Mode: Place a red flag on a cell you suspect contains a hidden landmine to prevent accidental detonation.
  • Read the Clues: A number (1 through 8) indicates exactly how many mines are hiding in the eight adjacent surrounding squares (horizontally, vertically, and diagonally).
  • Chord Clicking: Click on an already revealed numbered square whose surrounding flags equal its number to instantly uncover all remaining adjacent tiles!
  • Win Condition: Successfully uncover all safe squares without touching a single mine!

🧠 Top Minesweeper Solving Patterns

  • The 1-1 Corner Pattern: When a 1 sits in a corner, its only diagonal unrevealed neighbor must contain the mine!
  • The 1-2-1 Straight Wall: When you see a 1-2-1 sequence along a flat wall, the mines are guaranteed to hide directly under the two 1s, leaving the space below the 2 safe.
  • The 1-2-2-1 Pattern: Along a straight wall, the two mines will always be positioned beneath the middle 2s.
  • Safe Edge Openings: In Minesweeper Unlimited, your very first click is guaranteed to never be a mine, granting you an immediate foothold.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do the difficulty modes compare?

Beginner features a 9x9 grid with 10 mines (12.3% mine density). Intermediate expands to a 16x16 grid with 40 mines (15.6% density). Expert challenges you with a massive 30x16 battlefield containing 99 mines (20.6% density).

How do I flag mines on a mobile smartphone or tablet?

You can toggle the Flag Mode button directly below the board to switch your taps between digging tiles and planting flags, or long-press on any cell to drop a flag.

Is Minesweeper pure logic or does it require guessing?

Over 80% of situations can be resolved with pure deductive logic. For rare 50/50 end-game scenarios, make the move that opens the most informative area or leaves the fewest unresolved dependencies.
